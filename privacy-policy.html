FindIt — Privacy Policy

How FindIt handles your information, in plain language.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

What FindIt is

FindIt helps you keep track of what is stored in each box, tote, or container. You create containers, add items with photos and descriptions, and scan a QR code on a container to see what is inside. FindIt is made by Today’s Big Deals.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, which you use to sign in (directly, or with Google sign-in). Your password is stored securely by our sign-in provider and we never see it.
  • Your storage data: the containers and items you create — names, descriptions, quantities, locations, shelf labels, and capacity settings.
  • Photos: photos you add to containers and items. Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload and stored in a private storage area that only signed-in users can reach.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to show you your own containers and items, to let you share a container when you choose to, and to suggest a title and description for an item when you tap Suggest. We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

Sharing

If you share a container — with a 6-character access code or by email — the person you share with can see that container and add items to it. They cannot edit or delete your items, and they cannot share it further. Nobody else can see your containers: each person sees only their own.

Photos and AI suggestions

When you tap Suggest on a photo, the photo is sent to Google Gemini through our own server so Gemini can suggest a title and description for the item. The photo is used only for that suggestion. No advertising or tracking company receives your photos.

Subscriptions

FindIt is free for your first 10 containers, with unlimited items inside them. Adding an 11th container requires a $1.99 per month subscription, paid through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple handles the payment — FindIt never sees your card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple account settings.

Where your data lives

Your account and storage data are hosted by Supabase in the United States (East US region). Your data is protected by sign-in: only you (and people you share a container with) can see it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your containers, items, and photos for as long as your account exists, so the app works when you open it. You can delete your account — and all of your containers, items, and photos with it — at any time from inside the app: go to Settings and tap Delete account. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children’s privacy

FindIt is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at todaysbigdeals.co@gmail.com.
